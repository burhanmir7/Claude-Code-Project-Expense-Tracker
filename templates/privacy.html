Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a WISEX account, we collect basic information such as your name, email address, and password. As you use the Service, we also store the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — so that the app can function.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate and improve WISEX: authenticating your account, storing your expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and letting you filter your spending by time period. We do not use your expense data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

WISEX does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) solely to operate the Service, and any such providers are bound to handle your data securely.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@wisex.example.
